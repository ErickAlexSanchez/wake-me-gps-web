# WakeMeGPS logo

The mark in the app is concept 1, the radius: an amber ring and a center dot on night green. That ring is the alarm's radius.

Colors
- Night: #0e1412
- Amber: #ffb000
- Cream: #f4efe2
- Ink: #1c1914

Files
- logos/concept-1-radio/icon.svg is the app icon, the favicon, and the Play icon.
- horizontal.svg and vertical.svg place the name beside or under the mark.
- icon-mono-light.svg is the cream mark, for dark backgrounds.
- icon-mono-dark.svg is the ink mark, for light backgrounds.
- concept-2-pulso and concept-3-w are unused alternatives.

Leave clear space around the mark about the size of the center dot.
Keep the proportions and these colors. Play rounds the icon and draws the shadow.

Minimum size on screen: 48 px.
